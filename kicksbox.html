🛠️ Key Features to Consider Before Buying

Front-Drop vs. Side-Drop: Front-Drop doors open from the narrow front end—ideal for standard closet shelves and tight stacks. Side-Drop cases display the full profile of the shoe—popular for building decorative "sneaker walls."

Sizing (US Men's & Women's): Standard boxes fit up to US Men's 11 / Women's 12. Look for XL or Extra-Large sizes if you wear US Men's 13 to 15 or collect bulky high-tops.

Ventilation: Ensure boxes feature rear airflow vents to prevent moisture buildup, mold, or odors in enclosed plastic containers.

Door Seals: Magnetic closures provide a firmer seal against dust compared to snap-tab latches.
