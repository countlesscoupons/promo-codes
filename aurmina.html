What is Aurmina Liquid Water Purification & Deep Earth Minerals?

Aurmina is a revolutionary liquid water purification and refinement treatment made from ionic sulfate mineral salts extracted from biotite (a natural volcanic mineral and black mica). Rather than relying on physical filters or reverse osmosis that strip drinking water of natural nutrients, Aurmina utilizes an advanced chemical and mineral process to clarify, purify, and re-mineralize tap water into clean, living drinking water.

1. Coagulation, Flocculation & Binding

When added to water (1–2 teaspoons per gallon), Aurmina’s ionic sulfate minerals bind to charged contaminants including heavy metals, fluoride, pesticides, pharmaceuticals, VOCs, and chlorine.

2. Settling, Precipitation & Decanting

Bound contaminants clump together into visible sediment and settle to the bottom over 24 to 48 hours. Pour off or filter the crystal-clear purified water using a simple pitcher or decanter.

3. Bioavailable Trace Remineralization

Unlike reverse osmosis systems, Aurmina leaves behind and enriches your drinking water with essential bioavailable trace minerals like magnesium, calcium, potassium, and iron.

4. Primora Bio Sister Brand for Agriculture

Aurmina’s sister line, Primora Bio, applies this same mineral chemistry for soil regeneration, house plants, and agricultural irrigation to supercharge plant growth.
